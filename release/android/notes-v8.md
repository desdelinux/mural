# Android preview 8

Conversations start more gently and follow your answers more naturally. A quiet session now shows a small countdown beneath the orb before ending to save usage; speaking or typing lets you continue.

- Failed typed replies keep your draft so you can try again.
- Captions have cleaner spacing, and meanings use the complete caption. Very long captions show a clear limit message.
- Vocabulary and learning records retain valid evidence more reliably.
- Connection, credit and service errors offer clearer guidance. The reason a conversation ended stays visible.

Install **Mural-Android-direct-v8.apk** over your current direct-download preview to keep your history and settings. This release updates the direct download; it does not change the Play submission.
